# Draft

A simple, single-page fantasy football draft board. Rank players your own way, mark them off as they're picked, and always see the best player still available for the position you need.

## Why I built it

Most fantasy draft tools rely on the rankings from sites like ESPN or CBS. I did my own research and had my own rankings, but during a live draft, especially in the later rounds, it's hard to keep track of who's still on the board and who's left at the position you need. I built this page to solve that: a board of my own rankings that I update as players come off.

## How it works

1. Enter and rank your players using your own research.
2. During the draft, mark each player as picked when someone takes them.
3. Look at what's left on your board and choose the best available player at the position you need.
